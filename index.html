Client work

eRescue, a SaaS for beach lifeguards

I designed, sold and deployed eRescue alone. The first client is SMGBL, the beach-safety authority of the Landes: lifeguard teams use it on about 28 beaches, and I own the technical relationship, support and training. e-rescue.fr

  1. Discovery. Field sessions with lifeguards and coordinators to map how flags, logbooks, care reports and shifts were handled on paper.
  2. Demo and sale. Live demos for elected officials, then the first client signed.
  3. Rollout. Tablets set up in the lifeguard posts, with on-site training.
  4. Support. Releases and support through the season. Field feedback goes into the backlog (Jira, sprints).

What I built: a React Native tablet app for the posts, a Next.js dashboard for coordinators, real-time flags and incidents with Pusher, PostgreSQL on EU hosting (Neon) and Auth.js.

Data consulting for SMGBL: paper surveillance logs replaced by tablet capture, and attendance, incident and sea-weather data turned into statistics and a predictive attendance model presented to post chiefs and elected officials.
