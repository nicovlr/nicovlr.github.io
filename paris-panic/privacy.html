Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Paris Panic is a game for iPhone made by Nicolas Valerino, an independent developer ("we"). This policy explains what data is involved when you play and what you can do about it. Contact: nico.valerino@gmail.com.

What the game itself collects: nothing

There is no account, no sign-in and no server of ours. The game does not ask for your name, e-mail, contacts, photos, microphone or location. Your progress (records, medals, stripes, skills) and your settings are stored only on your iPhone. They are deleted when you delete the app, or when you use Settings › Erase progress in the game.

Advertising (Unity Ads)

The game is free and shows ads through Unity Ads, a service of Unity Technologies. Video ads are shown when you choose them (to continue a run or get a bonus), plus one short ad every two finished runs. To deliver, measure and protect these ads, the Unity Ads software in the app may collect directly, from your device:

  • device identifiers: the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking, and the app-specific identifier for vendors (IDFV);
  • technical data: device model, iOS version, language, approximate location derived from your IP address (country / region), network type;
  • ad interaction data: which ads were shown, viewed, completed or tapped; diagnostics such as ad loading errors.

Unity uses this data to show ads, to measure them (including Apple's SKAdNetwork attribution), to prevent fraud and, only with your permission, to show personalized ads. Unity acts as an independent controller of that data under its own privacy policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy. We do not receive data that identifies you; we only see aggregated ad statistics.

Your choices

  • Tracking (iOS): on the first launch, iOS asks whether Paris Panic may track you across apps and websites for advertising. If you refuse, the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalized. You can change this at any time in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): Unity asks for your consent before personalized advertising. You can review or withdraw it at any time with the privacy (ⓘ) button shown in Unity ads, where you can also ask Unity to access or delete the data it holds about you.
  • Everything: deleting the app removes all game data from your iPhone.

Children

Paris Panic is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

Retention, deletion and your rights

We keep no personal data. For data collected by Unity Ads, Unity's retention rules apply and requests can be made through the in-ad privacy button or Unity's privacy page. Under the GDPR you have rights of access, correction, deletion and objection; write to us for any question and we will help you reach Unity. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL: cnil.fr).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
