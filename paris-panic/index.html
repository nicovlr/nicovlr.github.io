A crowd marches down a postcard Paris boulevard from the Arc de Triomphe, and your cordon is the last line. Place units on the sidewalks, specialize them and hold as many waves as you can. Nobody really gets hurt: dispersed protesters run off to the café.

iPhone · plays offline · no account

Behind the game

  • Monetization

    Free-to-play with Unity Ads. Rewarded videos are opt-in (continue a lost run, or start the next one with extra cash) and interstitials are capped at one every two runs. A remove-ads purchase comes next.

  • Compliance

    App Tracking Transparency prompt before the ad SDK starts, privacy manifests, App Privacy labels and app-ads.txt. Details in the privacy policy.

  • Game design

    3 unit cards that branch into 7 specializations, 6 enemy types including a tractor boss, endless waves in 8-wave cycles, seasons that combine 3 streets with 5 rules, and a 12-skill progression tree.

  • Production

    Every 3D asset is generated by Blender scripts. One script runs the Unity build, automated playtests and the TestFlight upload.

Help

How do I change the language?
The game follows your iPhone's language. To pick another one for Paris Panic only: iPhone Settings › Apps › Paris Panic › Language (or in the game: Settings › Language).

Where is my progress saved?
On your iPhone only. Deleting the app erases it; you can also erase it in Settings › Erase progress.

About the ads
Video ads are only shown when you choose them (to continue a lost run or get a bonus for the next one), plus one short ad every two finished runs. On the first launch iOS asks whether apps may track you for ads: the game works the same whatever you answer, and you can change it any time in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
