Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Altivolt is a game for iPhone made by Nicolas Valerino, an independent developer ("we"). This policy explains what data is involved when you play and what you can do about it. Contact: nico.valerino@gmail.com.

What the game itself collects: nothing

There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no server of ours. The game does not ask for your name, e-mail, contacts, photos, microphone or location. Your save (coins, best distance, upgrades) and your settings are stored only on your iPhone. They are deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising (Unity Ads)

The game is free and shows a small banner ad through Unity Ads, a service of Unity Technologies, at the top of the title and results screens. No ad is shown during a run.

Altivolt asks Unity for non-personalized ads only: it tells the Unity Ads software that you have not consented to personalized advertising. The game never shows Apple's tracking prompt (App Tracking Transparency), so iOS does not give the advertising identifier (IDFA) to the game or to Unity.

To deliver, measure and protect these ads, the Unity Ads software in the app still receives some data directly from your device:

  • technical data: device model, iOS version, language and network type;
  • your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it (country / region);
  • app and ad interaction data: which ads were shown or tapped, and diagnostics such as ad loading errors;
  • identifiers that are not the advertising identifier, such as the app-specific identifier for vendors (IDFV) and an installation ID created by Unity's software, as described in Unity's privacy policy.

Unity uses this data to show ads, to measure them and to prevent fraud. Unity acts as an independent controller of that data under its own privacy policy: unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy. We do not receive data that identifies you; we only see aggregated ad statistics.

Your choices

  • Tracking (iOS): Altivolt never asks to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so the advertising identifier is never shared, whatever your setting in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Unity's data: Unity's privacy policy explains how to ask Unity for access to, or deletion of, the data its software received. Write to us if you need help reaching Unity.
  • Everything: deleting the app removes all game data from your iPhone.

No purchases, no data sales

Altivolt has no in-app purchases and never asks for payment details. We do not sell or rent any data about you; the game itself collects none.

Children

Altivolt is rated for all ages and children may play it, so we treat it as a mixed-audience app. The game itself collects no personal data from anyone, child or adult. In Unity's dashboard the app is declared as directed to children (mixed audience) under COPPA, and the game asks Unity for non-behavioural, non-personalised ads for every player: ads are chosen from the context only, never from a profile. The data the Unity Ads software receives is handled by Unity under its own privacy policy, which explains how Unity treats data about children.

Retention, deletion and your rights

Your save stays on your iPhone until you delete the app, and we keep no personal data. For data received by Unity Ads, Unity's retention rules apply and requests go to Unity, as explained in its privacy policy. Under the GDPR you have rights of access, correction, deletion and objection; write to us at nico.valerino@gmail.com for any question and we will help you reach Unity. You may also complain to your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL: cnil.fr).

Changes
